How liquidation works

  • Each liquidation takes one whole vault, the first in the list.
  • That vault is sold at the bonus discount and all of it repays debt. The part beyond what lifts HF to 1.00 is the fairness debt repayment, and it lifts HF on up to THF.
  • WBTC goes to the borrower only when a vault is worth more than all remaining debt. With this split that only happens on the last vault.
  • If HF is still below 1 after the first vault, the second vault goes right away.

What the numbers mean

  • THF is our split parameter: the HF right after the first vault is taken, if the liquidation happens at the expected HF. Aave's own target HF does not apply to this liquidation path.
  • Expected HF also sets the bonus through the Aave v4 curve: 4.99% just below 1.00, up to 5.55% at 0.90.
  • ↓ number in a cell: the deepest HF a liquidation can land at and still take only the first vault. Land any deeper and both vaults go.

Why THF 1.08 at HF 0.99

  • Aave liquidations land close to HF 1: median 0.997 on V3 over the last year, 0.998 on V4. For BTC, only the Oct 10 2025 crash went below 0.97.
  • After the first liquidation BTC can fall another 7.4% before the second vault is touched. That is roughly a 1 in 1,000 four hour BTC move (99.9th percentile, per LlamaRisk).
  • The first vault stays the smaller one up to CF 86%, so after a liquidation we only ever ask for less BTC than the user still has.

Parameters: launch CF 78%, max liquidation bonus 5.55%, bonus factor 90%, HF for max bonus 0.90. Liquidation data: our own on-chain analysis of Aave V3 (Ethereum Core, Sep 2025 to Sep 2026) and Aave V4 (since launch).
